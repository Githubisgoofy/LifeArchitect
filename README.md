# LifeArchitect v3.0

A BitLife-inspired life simulation game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

**Character Creation**
- Create your own character with name and gender selection
- Watch your avatar age from baby to elderly

**Career System**
- 7 different jobs (Janitor, Barista, Teacher, Engineer, Doctor, CEO, Actor)
- Career-specific salary requirements
- Choose a college major at 18 (Engineering, Business, Arts)

**Life Events**
- 👶 Have kids with your partner (-$5k, +30 happiness)
- 💰 Random inheritance opportunities
- 🚗 Random accidents (-15 health)
- 🎉 Retirement at 65
- 🎓 School & college system
- 💘 Dating & relationships
- 🏥 Therapy & plastic surgery

**Assets & Purchases**
- 🎫 Get a driver's license (70% pass rate)
- 🚗 Buy cars (requires license)
- 🏠 Buy houses
- 🐕 Get pets for happiness bonuses

**Gameplay**
- Crime system (rob, pickpocket, etc)
- Party, gym, and activity system
- Sound effects on every action
- Weighted random life events with cleaner balancing
- Better death system with cause of death
- Dark mode UI with smooth animations
- Live life snapshot panel (career, relationship, kids, fame, salary, education)
- Autosave after every state update

## How to Play

1. Open the game at: [▶️ Play LifeArchitect Live](https://githubisgoofy.github.io/LifeArchitect/)
2. Enter your name and choose your gender
3. Click **AGE UP** to progress through life
4. Use the 6 action buttons to make life choices:
   - 📚 School - Education & college
   - 💼 Jobs - Career selection
   - 💘 Love - Dating system
   - 🏠 Shop - Buy assets & license
   - 🏄 Activities - Party, gym, therapy
   - 😈 Crime - Risky money making

## Tips
- Get a driver's license before buying a car
- College majors give stat bonuses based on choice
- Have kids to increase happiness
- Manage your health or die young!
- Relationships can produce offspring
